- 2026-09-23 — Update after competitor review. Corrected the claim that only credit-risk products exist: named Proff Forvalt (user-picked comparison) and Valutico (automated peer recommendation for valuation, 2026), both verified. Added percentile display and a deterministic per-peer inclusion reason. Moved composite score, screening/rankings/"find similar" and free-form chat explicitly out of v1.
- 2026-09-23 — Access model reworked. Analysis now open without an account (Supabase anonymous sign-in, so one access model with RLS and audit intact); an account is needed to save, enter unfiled figures, export and share. Magic-link sign-in. Three fixed roles replaced by workspaces with owner/viewer and invitation by email; an adviser is a user with many workspaces. Protection against difference attacks dropped as a security objective: aggregates are built only from public filings and peers are shown by name, so there was nothing non-public to protect, and anonymous access makes per-user query tracking impossible anyway. Minimum group size kept as a quality threshold. Share links and a full valuation tool moved out of v1.
- 2026-09-23 — Consistency pass. "Aimed at the company" paragraph rewritten to match the competitor section, keeping ratios and kroner both in view. Unfiled figures belong to the workspace and are visible to its members, marked unaudited, compared against peers' latest filed year with the period gap stated. No fixed number of industries: coverage grows one industry at a time, each opened only once measured; companies outside coverage get their key figures but no peer comparison. "Understand where to start" no longer claims to judge what is addressable; persistence is decided by the engine. "Structured" claim corrected — only the latest-year summary is structured. Advisers made the primary user; managing directors and boards served in v1, investors later.
- 2026-09-23 — Description quality. Industry screening showed that at most about half of business descriptions distinguish the company (38–56 % by a generous proxy). The brief no longer claims descriptions can always carry peer selection: uninformative companies fall back to industry and size, and that is stated to the user. Credibility is reported separately for informative and uninformative descriptions.
- 2026-09-23 — Peer matching from two sources. The accounts are now read as a business-model fingerprint (cost of goods, inventory, capitalised intangibles, asset intensity, personnel cost in bands) for every company that files; the description adds what the numbers cannot. Each peer states what its match rests on. The user may describe the subject in a sentence, which takes precedence. Credibility is measured layer by layer. Peers are never selected on a benchmarked measure.
- 2026-09-23 — Key figures defined in docs/key-figures.md: about a dozen measures with revenue growth for context, EV/EBIT as the valuation basis, and a second decomposition separating pay level from productivity.
