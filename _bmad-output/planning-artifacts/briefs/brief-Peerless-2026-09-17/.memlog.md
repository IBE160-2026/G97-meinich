- 2026-09-23 — Update after competitor review. Corrected the claim that only credit-risk products exist: named Proff Forvalt (user-picked comparison) and Valutico (automated peer recommendation for valuation, 2026), both verified. Added percentile display and a deterministic per-peer inclusion reason. Moved composite score, screening/rankings/"find similar" and free-form chat explicitly out of v1.
- 2026-09-23 — Access model reworked. Analysis now open without an account (Supabase anonymous sign-in, so one access model with RLS and audit intact); an account is needed to save, enter unfiled figures, export and share. Magic-link sign-in. Three fixed roles replaced by workspaces with owner/viewer and invitation by email; an adviser is a user with many workspaces. Protection against difference attacks dropped as a security objective: aggregates are built only from public filings and peers are shown by name, so there was nothing non-public to protect, and anonymous access makes per-user query tracking impossible anyway. Minimum group size kept as a quality threshold. Share links and a full valuation tool moved out of v1.
