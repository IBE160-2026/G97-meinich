Analysis page: three layout directions

Visual identity is held fixed (palette, Inter with tabular numerals, markers, number format, vocabulary). Only the information structure differs. Each option opens on Oversikt; use the buttons, or click the tabs inside either frame, to see Nøkkeltall og gap. All companies and figures are fictional. In every distribution strip, better values are to the right, so lower-is-better figures are drawn mirrored.

A · Research note

Reads like the first page of a research report: one column, a summary block that states the finding before any navigation, and the explanation in prose right under the headline gap. Tabs sit below the summary, so the first screen is the conclusion and the tabs are the appendix. Shows Source Serif 4 on headings (falls back to Georgia here, since no fonts are loaded); the hero figure stays in the sans.

Phone: tabs become a horizontally scrollable strip (Utvikling carries a lock and a second line "Krever konto"); the key-figures table turns into stacked cards, one per key figure, with cost shares indented as a group.

Trade-off: once you scroll past the summary, the headline gap and peer count are gone; comparing two figures side by side is slower in cards than in a table.
