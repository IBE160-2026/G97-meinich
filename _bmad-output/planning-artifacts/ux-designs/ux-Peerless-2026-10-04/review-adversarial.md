# Adversarial review: DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md (v1)

Reviewer stance: sceptical sensor. Read on 2026-10-10 against the brief, the teacher's feedback (2026-10-06), PRD §6 and FR-73, AGENTS.md, `docs/key-figures.md` and `mockups/v1-analysis.html`.

## Verdict

The visual system is disciplined. The arithmetic in the flows checks out to the krone: 7,0 pp × 16 714 286 = 1 170 000; the four waterfall bars sum to 267 429; 14,0 pp × 12 518 082 = 1 752 531. The ✦ rule is sharper than in most student work. That is not where this spec fails. It fails where it meets the demo seed and the teacher's call for a minimal v1.

The happy path is specified in detail for a fictional company, Fjordkode, that a sensor will never see. The path a sensor is most likely to take is to type a real 62.100 orgnr into a fresh clone. That path rests on peer-selection stages that need OCR figures the seed does not hold, and the PRD and this spine disagree about what it says.

The analysis page is still four tabs, three chart types, three fold-outs, two segmented controls and fuzzy name search. Nothing tells a story developer which thin slice to build first. Several numbers sit side by side with different references (full convergence against 50 %, median against samlet, one share's median against every amount). Copy guards these, but the layout does not.

Grade risk today: good on process, exposed on functionality, scope and runnability.

**Counts:** 3 critical · 9 high · 10 medium · 4 low

## Findings

### Critical

- **[critical]** The analysis of any 62.100 company outside the demo seed is undefined, and the two specs contradict each other about it (EXPERIENCE State Patterns "62.100 company outside the demo seed"; PRD FR-73 "What any other company shows"). The PRD says document cells read "For få sammenlignbare"; the spine says "Ikke i demodataene". Worse, neither says what the **Peers tab** shows. Stage 4, the fingerprint (FR-10), runs on `varekostnad`, inventory and capitalised intangibles. All of these are OCR fields (`docs/key-figures.md` Source fields), and the subject and nearly every candidate lack them in the demo seed. So the funnel counts, "Forretningsmodell", "Grunnlag for treff" and "Hvorfor med" have no defined behaviour. This is the state shown for roughly 95 % of what the search box can reach. *Fix:* Specify an explicit "API-only peer group" mode: stages 1–3 run, stages 4–5 are shown as "Ikke kjørt – regnskapstall ikke i demodataene" with counts carried through, and every peer has the basis "Kun bransje og størrelse". Pick one string and edit the PRD or the spine so they agree. Mock the state.

- **[critical]** Widening the size band and excluding peers break in the demo seed (EXPERIENCE Size-band control, Peer list; FR-73 demo seed). Document figures are committed only for "the peer groups of A and B". The 0,33–3× and 0,25–4× steps pull in candidates with no document figures. Every OCR row then loses peers, the waterfall's common set can fall below 10, and the fingerprint cannot run on the newcomers. A sensor who clicks "Utvidet" on demo company A, the one control the brief calls "interrogate the peer group", gets a degraded analysis that nobody specified. *Fix:* Define the declared document set as the 0,25–4× candidate pool for A and B after stages 1–3, and make the FR-73 seed check assert exactly that. Add an acceptance case: A widened to 0,25–4× still yields a waterfall.

- **[critical]** Scope has crept back against the teacher's "lag en enkel, fungerende analyseside … tidlig" (EXPERIENCE Information Architecture, Component Patterns; DESIGN Components). v1 still carries four tabs. It has three chart grammars: the beeswarm, distribution strips, and a waterfall with separate desktop and phone renderings. It has three fold-outs (FR-26 names *two* decomposition views; the waterfall is a third), two segmented controls with disabled and median sub-states, fuzzy name search with keyboard results (the brief's front page "holds one field — the organisation number"), and a partly specified dark mode. Neither spine names a first buildable slice, so the epics will inherit everything at equal priority. *Fix:* Add a "Slice 0" section. One page at `/analyse/{orgnr}` for demo A: company header, the benchmark table with strips, kroner column at full convergence, the peer list with Hvorfor med, and the stored explanation. No tabs, no beeswarm, no waterfall, no Verdi control. Then slice 1 adds exclude/restore and the URL state, and slice 2 adds Verdi and the closable share. Move the beeswarm (the strip on the Driftsmargin row already shows the same distribution), the "Lønnsnivå eller produktivitet?" fold-out, dark mode and fuzzy name search to "if time allows within v1". At minimum, order them last in the spine.

### High

- **[high]** The same driftsmargin gap appears as two different "per år" amounts on adjacent tabs (EXPERIENCE Hero card vs Closable-share control). The Oversikt hero shows 1 170 000 kr per år; Verdi opens at 50 % and shows 585 000 kr as "Økt driftsresultat per år". A user who toggles tabs sees the headline number halve, and the small top note "Viser gap ved full tilnærming" will not prevent it. *Fix:* Either start Verdi at 100 % so the defaults agree, or make the hero show both figures, "1 170 000 kr ved full tilnærming · 585 000 kr ved valgt andel (50 %)". Specify which in the spine and add it to the vocabulary.

- **[high]** One cost share is judged against two different peer references on the same screen, and they can give opposite verdicts (Benchmark row cost-share rows vs Margin waterfall). The row compares Lønnskostnadsandel 66,9 % with the peer *median*, with a placement that may read "▲ Styrke". The fold-out directly beneath it shows "Lønn −501 428" in amber against *samlet*, 63,9 %. Add the margin's three references (median −3,2 %, samlet 0,5 %, beste fjerdedel 9,1 %) and Kari sees "above median, above samlet, 7 pp gap" plus a positive waterfall total (+267 429) under a 1 170 000 kr gap. *Fix:* Repeat the samlet value in the row subline whenever the fold-out is open. Spell out the case where median and samlet disagree in direction, with copy ("Mot medianen: styrke · mot peer-gruppen samlet: høyere"). Add Flow 4 figures that make the reader reconcile them.

- **[high]** The "Største gap i kroner" hero is not a ranking, and its empty case has no specification (EXPERIENCE Hero card, Small card; key-figures.md table). Only key figure 1 carries a *Profit* kroner translation, so "the largest kroner gap per year" is always driftsmargin by construction. If the company is at or above beste fjerdedel on margin, the spec does not say what the hero shows. A one-off capital amount in the `hero-amount` slot with "per år" would be wrong; so would an empty card. "Nest største gap" also ranks across receivables, payables and operating assets without a stated rule, and "Største styrke" has no ranking rule at all. *Fix:* Rename the hero to "Driftsmargin – gap i kroner" or state that the hero is always driftsmargin. Write the "no profit gap" state with copy. Define the ranking rule for the two small cards (kroner for gaps, percentile for strength) so it is testable.

- **[high]** "Til medianen (n %)" applies driftsmargin's n to every amount (EXPERIENCE Closable-share control; vocabulary "Median nås ved 42 % av gapet"). The control scales all amounts by one share, but n comes from driftsmargin only. At "Til medianen", working capital and operating assets do not reach *their* medians, yet the step's label says they do. The note "Medianen gjelder driftsmargin" sits only in the above-median state. *Fix:* Label the step "Driftsmargin til medianen (12 %)", or drop it and keep only the per-card median lines. Show "Medianen gjelder driftsmargin" in both states.

- **[high]** The explanation's own source line becomes false after any adjustment (EXPERIENCE Explanation card, State "Adjusted peer group", AI marking). The text is stored for the default group, and every figure in it "links to its calculation". After the user excludes two peers, the analysis recomputes and the text does not, yet the line "Hvert tall finnes i analysen" stays. Which calculation does a stale figure link to, the current group's or the default's? *Fix:* After an adjustment, collapse the explanation to its tag plus "Vis forklaringen for standard peer-gruppe". Drop the figure links, or make them open the default group's values labelled as such. Change the source line in that state.

- **[high]** AI claims without digits escape both ✦ granularity and the FR-53 test (AI marking; DESIGN AI marker; mock `about.ai.p3`). The test rejects figures absent from engine output. "Den største svakheten", "over medianen", "har vedvart" and "lavere enn" carry no digit, so a wrong direction or rank passes. "Persisted" gaps (brief, Solution) need the multi-year data that is stage 2, so the fixtures may already contain unverifiable claims. Meanwhile the mock's "Teksten vises da ikke" promises a *runtime* guard, but in v1 the test runs in CI against fixtures (FR-53). *Fix:* Restrict the explanation template to engine-provided slots for direction and rank: the model receives "{figure} is a weakness, rank 1", and the test also checks direction words against engine output. Reword `about.ai.p3` to "En automatisk test sjekker hver lagrede tekst …". State in the spine that v1 explanations make no persistence claims.

- **[high]** The trust claim of "trace any kroner amount" stops at half the formula (EXPERIENCE Provenance drill-down; mock `ve.trace.*`). The mocked trace shows the subject's `driftsresultat` and `sumDriftsinntekter` only. The beste fjerdedel, the other operand of every amount, is not traced: which peers, which values, which quartile method. For OCR figures, "filed values" ends at a field name, because the scanned page is not available. The mock's "Datakvalitet: avstemt mot innsendt årsregnskap" is false for an outside-seed company, whose API figures were never reconciled against a document. The trace formula also includes "× andel som lukkes" when opened from the hero, which is always at 100 %. *Fix:* Add a step that names the target: "Beste fjerdedel 9,1 % = øvre kvartil av 23 verdier, Vis verdiene". Make the data-quality line conditional, with a third variant "ikke avstemt – regnskapet er ikke i demodataene". Fix the share to the context the trace was opened from.

- **[high]** No acceptance figures exist for anything a sensor can run (Key Flows; Open questions "Demo companies A and B"). Every flow figure belongs to fictional Fjordkode or Anders's prospect. Demo A, B and C are unnamed, so the 214 → 131 → 88 → 31 → 23 funnel, 1 170 000 kr and 267 429 kr cannot be checked against the seed, and the not-found state's "links to demo companies A and B" cannot be written. *Fix:* Pick A, B and C now. Re-run UJ-1 and Flow 4 on A with its real filed figures as the hand-calculated reference case (AGENTS.md Testing), and put the real numbers in the spine. Keep Fjordkode only as illustration, marked as such.

- **[high]** Several URL-state decisions that block stories are still open (IA "[ASSUMPTION] How the active tab is addressed"; Interaction Primitives "replace vs push not decided"). FR-69 is load-bearing and testable only once the schema is fixed: tab path or query, how excluded peers are encoded (an orgnr list can run to 9 × n characters), band step, share, multiple, and history behaviour. *Fix:* Decide and write the schema in the spine, for example `/analyse/{orgnr}/{tab}?ex=…&band=1&andel=50&mult=`, with replace for the share and multiple and push for exclusions and the tab.

### Medium

- **[medium]** The FR-4 uncovered state is reachable for one company only, and the not-found copy contradicts coverage (State "Orgnr not in the database"; FR-73 seed). The seed holds 62.100 plus demo C. Any other 69.202 or 43.210 orgnr gets "not in database", not the promised API figures and statement. So does any 62.100 company below five employees, which is then told "Foreløpig dekker vi programmeringstjenester (62.100)" about its own industry. The demo links also omit C. *Fix:* Split the copy: "Ikke i Peerless' datasett. Peerless har bare selskaper i 62.100 med minst fem ansatte, og ett eksempel fra en annen bransje." Link A, B *and* C.

- **[medium]** Mirrored strips have no value axis (DESIGN Distribution strip; mock `gap.col.dist`). For lower-is-better rows the company dot sits right of the median while the adjacent numeric cell is *higher* (66,9 % vs 63,9 %). Beste fjerdedel, the lower quartile, sits on the right. "Bedre →" is in the column head only, and nothing says how no-direction rows and the check row are oriented. *Fix:* Put a per-row micro-label "lavere er bedre ←→" or min and max values at the strip ends. State that no-direction and check rows are drawn unmirrored, with no "bedre" label.

- **[medium]** The rounded label of "Til medianen (12 %)" and the amount do not multiply (UJ-2 step 4). 0,12 × 1 752 531 = 210 304, not 212 807. The amount uses the exact share, 1,7/14,0 = 12,14 %. A sensor checking the arithmetic will flag it. *Fix:* State that the step uses the exact share, label it with one decimal or "≈ 12 %", and give the trace the exact share.

- **[medium]** Several states have no copy, so a story cannot be accepted. Missing are: no stored explanation ("a plain statement"); waterfall below floor or with a missing component ("count stated"); hero with no profit gap; the Verdi working-capital card when only payables have a target (is the payables half shown, or the whole card withheld?); Verdi for an outside-seed company; and the FR-71, no-match, rate-limit, lookup-error and loading strings, which are all still `[ASSUMPTION]`. *Fix:* Add one row per state with final bokmål copy to messages-v1.nb.json, and remove the `[ASSUMPTION]` tags or list them as story blockers.

- **[medium]** The data-quality flag is hidden in a drill-down, against the spine's own rule (EXPERIENCE Provenance "The data quality flag lives here, not as a badge"; DESIGN Brand "a caveat is set as plainly as a result, never hidden in a tooltip"). FR-57's per-filing flag, including a *peer's* flag, is visible only after opening a kroner trace. *Fix:* Add an inline slate subline in the company header and the peer row when a filing is not fully reconciled.

- **[medium]** The licence and credit for document figures are not specified (State "Source attribution"; mock `about.sources.p2`). The brief says the register states no licence for the filed documents. The spine promises "the separate credit for document figures" and gives no string. The footer's "Data fra Brønnøysundregistrene" covers both sources without distinguishing them. *Fix:* Write the document-figure paragraph, including that only a declared sample is committed and why. Link it from the "Ikke i demodataene" tooltip.

- **[medium]** AI influence on inclusion is unmarked (AI marking; Funnel). "Hvorfor med" correctly carries no ✦, but whether a peer is in the group at all depended on stage-5 classification, and the "Klassifisering" funnel count is model output without ✦. Companies the model removed are invisible. *Fix:* Put ✦ on the "Klassifisering" stage label. Add an "n tatt ut av KI-klassifiseringen – vis" disclosure to the funnel.

- **[medium]** The phone flow is partly decorative (Flow 3 `[ASSUMPTION]`; Beeswarm; Benchmark row at 375). Twenty-three packed dots at about 340px cannot meet the spine's own 44px tap target, so "taps a dot, naming a peer he knows" is unlikely. The tap-expanded benchmark row must also contain the waterfall, two factor pairs and the drill-down, nested inside a table row. That is not mocked, and the share control wraps six steps onto two rows. *Fix:* At 375, replace dot tapping with the compact peer list sorted by value, highlighting the company. Make fold-outs open full-width *below* the row group, not inside it. Mock one phone fold-out.

- **[medium]** The about page can contradict v1's own gate (State "Measurement pending"; PRD §6.1 "offered only once … its peer selection has been measured"). If v1 ships with "Målingen pågår", the industry is offered before its measurement, against the PRD. *Fix:* Either make published measurement a v1 acceptance criterion, or reword §6.1 and say so on the about page.

- **[medium]** "A large residual is a data-quality signal" has no threshold (Margin waterfall). It is untestable, and the user gets no cue. In Flow 4 the residual is 317 572 kr (1,9 pp); is that large? *Fix:* Define a threshold, for example |residual| > 2 pp or more than 50 % of |total|, and a slate note for when it is crossed. Otherwise drop the claim.
