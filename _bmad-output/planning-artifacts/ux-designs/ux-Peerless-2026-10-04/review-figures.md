# Review: money and figures in the v1 UX spines

Reviewed 2026-10-10 against `docs/key-figures.md` (authoritative), `AGENTS.md`, the PRD (UJ-1, UJ-2, FR-21–FR-32, FR-52, FR-53, FR-60, FR-73), `DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, `mockups/review-notes-v1.md` and the data/arithmetic block of `mockups/v1-analysis.html` (lines 655–762, 1036–1060). Review only; nothing else edited.

## Verdict

**The arithmetic holds; the rules around it have gaps.** Every kroner amount, share, pp gap, multiple and count in the two spines recomputes to the stated value from the exact inputs. The waterfall sums exactly, largest-remainder rounding is applied correctly, and nothing is computed on 16,7 mill. kr. The mock's BigInt engine reproduces the same values. Two things are not right. First, the "Til medianen" step is not defined in `key-figures.md`, and the amount the spine quotes for it (212 807 kr) is reached only if the step uses the exact share; with the displayed 12 % it is 210 304 kr. Second, after the user adjusts the peer group, the stored explanation can carry figures that no longer appear on screen. Several journey figures also assume the default group in steps that come after the user has excluded peers.

Counts: **critical 0 · high 2 · medium 7 · low 11**.

## Findings

### High

- **[high]** "Til medianen" is not defined in `key-figures.md`, and its arithmetic depends on an undocumented choice (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Closable-share control", Vocabulary "Closable share", State Patterns "Above/Below median", UJ-2.4; DESIGN Components "Closable-share control"; mock `stepSeg`/`profitAt(s)` lines 676, 1039–1043). `key-figures.md` says the median "is shown … and marked on the control … but it never enters this arithmetic", and FR-31 says only that the median is *marked*. The spine makes it a selectable step whose share is s = (median − r)/(T − r). The resulting amount is (median − r) × revenue, so the median does enter the kroner arithmetic, just through s. The spine's 212 807 kr is right only with the exact s (0,12142857). The step is labelled "12 %", and the mock's `profitAt` takes a whole-number percent. Applied that way it gives **210 304 kr** and stops short of the median. Two more things are undefined: the step takes its s from driftsmargin but is applied to all amount cards, and nothing says what happens when the margin is below the floor or exactly at the median. *Fix:* Add the step to `key-figures.md` "From gap to kroner" in the same commit. Define s_median = (median − r)/(T − r) for driftsmargin, kept exact (decimal) and never rounded to the displayed percent. State that it is a position of the control, not a target, and that T stays the favourable quartile. Say it applies to every amount card, and that its label refers to driftsmargin only. The spine already says this with "Medianen gjelder driftsmargin", so keep that line mandatory. Disable the step when r ≥ median or when driftsmargin is below the floor. Change the mock's `profitAt`/`opAssetAt` to take a rational s, and add an engine test that the uplift at s_median equals (median − r) × revenue exactly.

- **[high]** After an adjustment, the explanation can carry figures that are not on screen, and its source line then makes a false claim (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Explanation card", State Patterns "Adjusted peer group", AI marking last bullet; UJ-1.3; mock `explCard(adjusted)` line 909). In v1 an adjusted group shows the default group's stored text. Once two peers are excluded, its −3,2 %, 9,1 %, 7,0 pp, 1 170 000 kr, 0,5 % and the samlet shares are no longer the figures the page shows. FR-52 says "every figure in the text links back to its calculation", but that calculation is no longer rendered. The source line "Hvert tall finnes i analysen" is then untrue. *Fix:* Specify that in the adjusted state each linked figure opens the **default group's** calculation, labelled as such ("standard peer-gruppe"), or that the figures are unlinked and visibly marked as belonging to the default group. Swap the source line for an adjusted-state variant, for example "Tallene i teksten gjelder standard peer-gruppe." Add a test that fails if an adjusted-state explanation links to a recomputed value.

### Medium

- **[medium]** The journey figures assume the default group after the journey has already excluded peers (EXPERIENCE Key Flows intro "Figures are for the default peer group", UJ-1.3→UJ-1.5, Flow 4 "after UJ-1"; Mock discrepancy 7). In UJ-1, Kari excludes two peers in step 3, and every figure recomputes. Step 5 then quotes the median −3,2 %, the beste fjerdedel 9,1 %, "bedre enn 61 %" and 1 170 000 kr, all of which are default-group (23) values. Flow 4 runs on the same URL and quotes the waterfall over P = 22. The mock shows the opposite: its 23 rows are what remains after the exclusions, and the default would be 25. A sensor cannot tell which group produced which number. *Fix:* Either move the exclusion after the climax in UJ-1 and start Flow 4 from "Tilbakestill til standard", or recompute the climax and waterfall figures for the 21-peer group. Fix the mock so that the default 23 includes the 2 that are excluded.

- **[medium]** "Nest største gap" ranks a one-off capital amount against a per-year profit amount, and the hero is undefined when driftsmargin has no kroner (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Hero card", "Small card"; DESIGN "Hero card", `hero-amount`). Only (1) yields an amount per year; (9), (10) and (11) are one-off capital. "Largest kroner gap per year" can therefore only ever be driftsmargin. If driftsmargin is a strength or below the floor, the spine does not say what the hero shows. "Nest største" then compares 412 000 kr (one-off) with 1 170 000 kr per year as if they were the same kind of number, which invites the reader to add them into a 1,58 mill. kr "total gap". *Fix:* Make the hero explicitly "Økt driftsresultat per år" (driftsmargin only), with a defined state when it has no amount. Rename the small card "Største kapitalgap" (largest of 9+10 and 11, one-off), ranked only against capital. Keep the mock line "Ikke per år, og legges ikke til resultatet" mandatory in the spine.

- **[medium]** Working capital released is undefined when only one of (9) and (10) has a target (EXPERIENCE "Amount card"; mock line 1056 `ve.amt.wcNa`). FR-22 is per figure. In the flows, receivable days are below the floor (7 of 10), and the mock withholds the whole working-capital card ("Ikke beregnet – for få sammenlignbare på kundefordringsdager"). That hides a valid payable-days amount. The opposite choice, showing (10) alone under "Frigjort arbeidskapital", would present a partial sum as the total. *Fix:* Decide and write it into `key-figures.md`. Recommended: show the parts as separate lines (receivable days / payable days) with a total only when both exist, and name the missing part in the total's place. The mock also omits payable days (Mock discrepancy 6), so the case is never drawn.

- **[medium]** The 412 000 kr capital amount does not say which closable share it was computed at, and its label is ambiguous (EXPERIENCE Vocabulary "Capital amount: Frigjort kapital: 412 000 kr (engangsbeløp)", Component Patterns "Small card"; Numbers and their context row "Kroner gap … the step it was computed at"). The Oversikt card shows 412 000 at full convergence without saying so, while Verdi shows 206 000 at 50 %. The generic label "Frigjort kapital" also fits "Frigjort arbeidskapital", which is a different amount that must never be added to it. *Fix:* Label it "Frigjort kapital i driftseiendeler", as on the Verdi card, and add "ved full tilnærming". Extend the top note "Viser gap ved full tilnærming" to the small cards.

- **[medium]** The two-bar subtotal "1 069 714 kr" has no source on screen (EXPERIENCE Flow 4.4). Lønn plus andre driftskostnader is not a waterfall bar, not the waterfall total, and not in "Vis tallene". If the explanation, or any copy, states it, FR-53's containment test fails or has to be loosened. *Fix:* Remove the number from the flow ("lønn og andre driftskostnader trekker ned mer enn varekostnaden trekker opp"). If the subtotal is wanted, make it an engine output shown in "Vis tallene" with its own calculation.

- **[medium]** The decomposition factors have no stated floor or count (EXPERIENCE "Factor pair", Flow 4.5–4.6; mock `DIST.turn` has no `n`). Totalkapitalens omløpshastighet, revenue per FTE and personnel cost per FTE are compared with their own medians. The first is not a numbered key figure, and the FTE pair depends on OCR `aarsverk`. Nothing says each factor's median needs 10 defined peers, or that the count is shown. *Fix:* State that each factor is gated by FR-22 on its own, shows its count, and shows the subject's value with "For få sammenlignbare (n av 10)" when below the floor. Add `sumDriftsinntekter / sumEiendeler` to `key-figures.md` as a named derived factor.

- **[medium]** The waterfall's reference and the margin row's kroner cell sit side by side with two different "margin gaps" (EXPERIENCE "Benchmark row", "Fold-out", Flow 4.2; mock line 1031). Under the Driftsmargin row, the gap cell (1 170 000 kr per year, against beste fjerdedel) sits directly above the fold-out lead "Forskjellen på 1,6 pp er 267 429 kr" (against samlet). The reference tag is the only thing separating them. A reader comparing 7,0 pp with 1,6 pp, or adding 267 429 to 1 170 000, has no explicit barrier. *Fix:* Keep the tag, and also print the "gap.wf.never" sentence ("legges aldri til økt driftsresultat") as a required element in the spine, directly above the waterfall at both widths, not only in the mock.

### Low

- **[low] L1** The percentile is rounded to whole percent, against the one-decimal rule (EXPERIENCE Vocabulary "Plassering — bedre enn 34 %", UJ-1.5 "bedre enn 61 %"; `key-figures.md` Arithmetic "percentages to one decimal"). The exact value is 60,87 %. *Fix:* Either show "bedre enn 60,9 %", or add an explicit exception for Plassering to `key-figures.md` and to the Number formatting table.
- **[low] L2** "42 %" appears unqualified in the Vocabulary and State Patterns ("Til medianen (42 %)", "Median nås ved 42 % av gapet"). Its inputs (1,0 / 4,0 / 8,1 %) belong to a separate example that does not match the 62.100 distribution, while UJ-2 gives 12 % for the same concept. *Fix:* Use UJ-2's 12 % throughout, or state the example inputs wherever 42 % appears.
- **[low] L3** Amounts are phrased as computed on rounded inputs (UJ-2.3 "14,0 pp × 12 518 082 kr", UJ-2.4 "1,7 pp × 12 518 082 kr", Flow 4.3 "1,6 pp × 16 714 286 kr"). The values match the exact result only because the invented inputs sit on round shares, and UJ-2 never gives a `driftsresultat`. *Fix:* Give UJ-2's `driftsresultat` (−613 386 kr reproduces both amounts), and phrase the checks as (T − driftsresultat/revenue) × revenue.
- **[low] L4** Peer counts disagree. Oversikt says "Se alle 23"; the 375 peer list says "Vis alle 25" (EXPERIENCE Responsive table, "Peer list"). *Fix:* "Vis alle 23" for the default group, or define the count as the active ones plus those the user excluded.
- **[low] L5** The samlet margin does not reconcile with the listed peer rows. Weighting the 22 displayed margins by revenue gives 0,471 %, which would make the start bar 78 741 kr instead of 83 571. This is within display rounding, but a sensor recomputing from the peer list will not land on 0,5 % (review-notes "Peer-gruppen samlet"). *Fix:* Make the invented sums consistent with the listed rows, or state in "Vis tallene" that the aggregate comes from unrounded filings.
- **[low] L6** The waterfall start bar and signed rounding are not defined (EXPERIENCE Number formatting last line; `key-figures.md` "Rounding"). The mock sets start = driftsresultat − displayed total and throws an error if that differs from round(M × revenue). With half-away-from-zero ties it can differ by 1 kr. "Largest remainder" is also not defined for negative bars (the mock uses the signed floor). *Fix:* In `key-figures.md`, define start := `driftsresultat` − displayed total, and floor toward −∞ before distributing the remainders.
- **[low] L7** The visible EV product may not multiply out (EXPERIENCE "Multiple field" "with the visible product"; mock `ve.ev.calc`). EV is rounded from the exact uplift, but the product shows the rounded uplift. At a multiple like 6,5 the displayed "a × m = v" can be a few kroner off. Decimal input is also unspecified (comma, tenths). *Fix:* Specify which is shown. Recommended: v = round(exact uplift × m), with the line marked "≈" or the product shown from the exact uplift. State the accepted precision of the multiple.
- **[low] L8** The context rule does not cover every number (EXPERIENCE "Numbers and their context"). Funnel counts, peer revenues and "omsetning 1,2× din", the size-band revenue range, the EV amount, the multiple, the "Til medianen" share and the waterfall's start and end bars are not in the table. *Fix:* Add rows. Counts and peer revenues are identification, like header revenue; EV's context is the entered multiple and the uplift; the start and end bars carry "margin 0,5 %" and "margin 2,1 %".
- **[low] L9** "48 dager" cannot be checked, because `kundefordringer` and `salgsinntekt` are not given in review-notes. *Fix:* Add them to the inputs table.
- **[low] L10** The PRD and the spine disagree on the outside-seed cell. FR-73 says a 62.100 company outside the demo seed shows "For få sammenlignbare" for document figures; the spine shows "Ikke i demodataene" (decided). *Fix:* Update FR-73 to match the decision.
- **[low] L11** The PRD journeys put the gap in % rather than pp (PRD UJ-1 "7.0 % × 16.7 m", UJ-2 "14.0 % × 12.5 m"), and compute on rounded revenue. The spine correctly uses pp and exact revenue. *Fix:* Change the PRD to "7,0 pp × 16 714 286 kr" and "14,0 pp × 12 518 082 kr".

- The target is beste fjerdedel at every s; s scales (T − r); no kroner at s = 0 (EXP "Closable-share control", mock `ve.ctl.sub`).
