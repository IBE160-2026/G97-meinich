**Date:** 2026-11-22
**Tool:** Claude Code (Opus 5.5), the BMAD session; no subagent
**Story / issue:** issue #71, branch `story/71-next-16-4-6` from `origin/main` (`50ac3e2`)
**Result:** commits `20f6ad8` (the pin), `c2ce742` (Story 5.7's audit AC), `28b5740` (AI log) and `af5999d` (zod 4.6.7 in the lockfile, 2026-11-24); the PR closes #71.

## Goal

Upgrade Next.js as its own PR before Story 5.4, so the build's security check stops failing (`SecurityFatalError`, every story from 3.2 to 3.8). Pin `next` and `eslint-config-next` to the latest 16.4.x patch. Leave zod and minimatch unless a normal upgrade of a direct dependency resolves them, with no `pnpm overrides`. Verify everything one step at a time.

## What came back, and what was done with it

- **The audit, checked first.** It confirmed the review session's count on `main`: seven moderate advisories in `next` 16.4.2, all `<=16.4.4` and fixed in `>=16.4.5`. Besides them came zod (high, GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5) and minimatch (moderate, GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249).
- **16.4.6** was published 2026-11-09 and is the latest 16.4.x. `eslint-config-next` 16.4.6 pins `@next/eslint-plugin-next` to the same version. The lockfile changed only the Next.js family, and no release-age exemption was needed.
- **zod and minimatch were not resolved in the first pass, by rule.**
  - zod is a peer of `@anthropic-ai/sdk` and a dependency of `eslint-plugin-react-hooks`, both `^3.25.0 || ^4.0.0`. That range already admits the fixed 4.6.6, and the latest SDK keeps it.
  - minimatch comes through `@typescript-eslint/typescript-estree` 8.71.1. Version 8.73.0 uses `^11.0.1`.
  - Only refreshing those transitive lockfile entries would move them. That is neither a direct upgrade nor an override, so it was left for Meinich to decide.
  - Neither package reaches the request path: the SDK is imported only by `scripts/classify/model-client.ts`, and the built `.next/server` holds no zod.

## 2026-11-24: zod moved, minimatch left

The prompt was written by the review session and pasted in by Meinich. The review session had checked the `package.json` and lockfile diff (only the Next.js packages) and the claim that the build's security check stopped in every story from 3.2 to 3.8 (the session files for 3.2–3.7 and the 3.8 spec). The prompt, verbatim, shortened:

> 1. zod: oppdater bare zod-oppføringen i lockfila til 4.6.7. Den ligger innenfor ^3.25.0 || ^4.0.0, så det er ingen override og ingen endring i package.json. […] Begrunnelse: zod har den eneste sårbarheten med høy grad, og pnpm classify i Epic 4 tolker modellsvarene med den.
> 2. minimatch blir stående. […] den bare brukes av ESLint og ville krevd at hele @typescript-eslint-familien oppdateres.
> 3. Session-fila: skriv at prompten ble skrevet av review-sesjonen og limt inn av meg.
>
> Push så branchen og åpne PR-en med pr71.md som tekst. Ikke poster noe på #71 ennå.

- **zod:** `pnpm update zod` (depth unlimited by default) moved only zod, 4.6.5 to 4.6.7, the latest release, in `af5999d`. The other changed lines are the peer suffix `(zod@4.6.7)` in the keys of `@anthropic-ai/sdk` 1.3.0 and `zod-validation-error` 4.0.2, whose versions did not change. `package.json` is unchanged.
- **Re-run:** `check:pins`, lint and typecheck clean; `pnpm test --maxWorkers=2` 2 086; `pnpm build` passed at the first attempt; `pnpm audit` shows one moderate advisory, minimatch, and none in zod or `next`.
- **minimatch** stays: only ESLint uses it, and moving it would mean upgrading the whole `@typescript-eslint` family. The PR text and the AI log say so. Nothing was posted on #71.
